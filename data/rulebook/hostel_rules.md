# 6. Hostel Rules

## 6.1 Hostel Admission and Allotment

Hostel accommodation is available to Registered Students on application to the Hostel Office, subject to availability of seats and payment of the prescribed hostel fee under Section 4.1. Allotment of hostel rooms is made by the Warden on the basis of criteria notified at the commencement of each Academic Year, which may include distance of the student's home address from the University, year of study, and academic standing. A student allotted hostel accommodation is required to complete check-in formalities within one week of the commencement of the semester, failing which the allotment is liable to be cancelled and offered to a waitlisted applicant.

## 6.2 General Hostel Conduct

Hostel residents are required to maintain cleanliness and order in their rooms and common areas, and to refrain from causing damage to hostel property. Possession of prohibited items, including electrical appliances not approved by the Hostel Office, alcohol, and any substance prohibited by law, is not permitted within hostel premises. Guests, including visiting family members, may be received in the designated common visiting area during notified visiting hours, upon prior intimation to the hostel warden on duty; overnight stay by guests within student rooms is not permitted under any circumstance.

## 6.3 Hostel Curfew

Hostel residents must return to the hostel by 10:00 PM, and entry after 10:00 PM is considered a curfew violation. The main hostel gate is secured at this time, and residents are expected to plan their return from academic, co-curricular, or personal engagements accordingly.

## 6.4 Late Entry Provisions

Hostel residents may enter the hostel premises until 11:00 PM, and entry before 11:00 PM is not considered a curfew violation. A resident entering the hostel during this period is required to record their entry with the security personnel on duty at the main gate.

## 6.5 Leave from Hostel

A hostel resident intending to be absent from the hostel overnight, whether for travel to their home town or otherwise, must submit a leave application to the Warden prior to departure, specifying the duration and destination of the leave. Outstation leave exceeding three days requires the additional written consent of the student's parent or guardian, submitted along with the leave application. A resident who fails to return by the date specified in an approved leave application without prior extension is required to report to the Warden immediately upon return and may be required to furnish an explanation in writing.

## 6.6 Disciplinary Action for Hostel Violations

A violation of these hostel rules, including curfew violations under Section 6.3, unauthorized possession of prohibited items under Section 6.2, or failure to return from leave under Section 6.5, is reported by the Warden to the Disciplinary Committee constituted under Section 7.2, and is dealt with in accordance with the disciplinary procedure prescribed therein. A resident found to have committed a hostel violation may, depending on the severity and frequency of the violation, be issued a written warning, required to vacate the hostel for the remainder of the semester, or, in cases of serious or repeated violation, be permanently debarred from hostel accommodation for the remainder of their program.

## 6.7 Room Inspections

The Hostel Office conducts periodic inspections of hostel rooms, ordinarily once each month, to verify compliance with the general hostel conduct rules under Section 6.2, including the absence of prohibited items and the maintenance of cleanliness. Inspections are ordinarily conducted during notified hours with prior general notice to residents of the hostel block, save where the Warden has reasonable grounds to conduct an unannounced inspection in response to a specific complaint or safety concern.

## 6.8 Maintenance Complaints

A hostel resident who identifies a maintenance issue within their room or in common areas, including electrical, plumbing, or furniture-related issues, may report the issue to the Hostel Office through the maintenance request facility on the student portal or by written complaint to the Warden. The Hostel Office is required to acknowledge a maintenance complaint within two working days and to undertake reasonable efforts to resolve the reported issue within a period appropriate to its nature and severity, with urgent safety-related complaints being prioritized.

## 6.9 Mess Rules

Every hostel resident is required to subscribe to the hostel mess facility for the duration of their stay, and mess charges are included within the hostel fee notified under Section 4.1. Mess timings are notified by the Hostel Office at the commencement of each semester and are displayed at the mess premises. A resident wishing to avail a temporary exemption from mess charges on account of extended absence exceeding seven consecutive days must apply to the Hostel Office in advance, accompanied by the leave application submitted under Section 6.5.

## 6.10 Prohibited Activities and Property Damage

In addition to the conduct rules specified under Section 6.2, residents are prohibited from engaging in any activity within hostel premises that endangers the safety of other residents, including but not limited to unauthorized use of electrical equipment, tampering with fire safety equipment, and organizing gatherings that disturb other residents during designated quiet hours. A resident found responsible for damage to hostel property, whether within their own room or in common areas, is liable to bear the cost of repair or replacement, as assessed by the Hostel Office, in addition to any disciplinary action that may be initiated under Section 6.6.

## 6.11 Room Checkout

A hostel resident vacating hostel accommodation at the end of an academic year, or upon earlier discontinuation of their program, is required to complete the room checkout procedure with the Hostel Office, which includes return of room keys, settlement of any outstanding dues, and inspection of the room by hostel staff to assess condition. A resident who fails to complete the checkout procedure prior to departure remains liable for hostel fees and any assessed damages until the checkout procedure is completed.

## 6.12 Common Area Usage

Common areas within the hostel, including the reading room, recreation room, and common kitchen where provided, are available to all residents of the concerned hostel block during hours notified by the Hostel Office, and are to be used in a manner that does not inconvenience other residents. Reservation of a common area for a private or group event requires prior written permission from the Warden, and any resident found to have used a common area in violation of notified hours or without permission may be subject to disciplinary action under Section 6.6.
