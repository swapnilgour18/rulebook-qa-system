# 1. Academic Structure and Calendar

## 1.1 Programs Offered and Credit Structure

MediTech University offers undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs across its constituent schools. Each undergraduate program is organized on a credit-based semester system, with a standard full-time course load of 20 to 24 credits per semester. A student registered for a program is referred to throughout these regulations as a "Registered Student." The Academic Year 2026–27 comprises two regular semesters — the Odd Semester and the Even Semester — and an optional Summer Term intended primarily for backlog clearance and credit improvement.

Each semester is nominally sixteen weeks in duration, inclusive of instructional weeks, internal assessment weeks, and the end-semester examination period. The Summer Term, where offered, is condensed to eight weeks and is subject to approval by the concerned School Dean.

## 1.2 Academic Calendar

The Academic Calendar for 2026–27 is notified by the Office of the Registrar prior to the commencement of each semester and is binding on all Registered Students, faculty, and administrative staff unless expressly amended by the Competent Authority. For the Odd Semester 2026, instruction commences on 1 August 2026 and concludes on 15 November 2026, followed by an internal assessment week and end-semester examinations scheduled between 1 December 2026 and 20 December 2026. Winter vacation extends from 21 December 2026 to 4 January 2027.

The Even Semester 2027 commences on 5 January 2027 and concludes on 20 April 2027, with end-semester examinations conducted between 1 May 2027 and 20 May 2027. Summer vacation follows from 21 May 2027 through 31 July 2027, during which the optional Summer Term, if notified, is conducted.

## 1.3 Definitions

For the purposes of this rulebook and all constituent policy documents issued under it, the following definitions apply uniformly:

"Registered Student" means any individual who has completed admission formalities and fee payment for the relevant semester and whose registration has not been cancelled, suspended, or withdrawn.

"Competent Authority" means the Vice-Chancellor, or such officer, committee, or body as the Vice-Chancellor may designate in writing to exercise a specific power under these regulations.

"Academic Session" means either the Odd Semester or the Even Semester of a given Academic Year, as the context requires.

"School" means an academic constituent unit of the University headed by a Dean, responsible for administering one or more programs.

"Head of Department" means the officer responsible for academic and administrative supervision of a department within a School.

## 1.4 Registration and Course Requirements

Every student is required to complete academic registration for each semester within the registration window notified in the Academic Calendar, ordinarily during the first week of instruction. Registration is contingent upon clearance of all fee dues for the preceding semester and absence of any active disciplinary debarment. A student who fails to register within the notified window may be granted late registration by the Head of Department upon payment of the late registration fee, provided the request is made no later than two weeks after the close of the regular registration window.

Course requirements for each program, including core courses, elective courses, and minimum credit thresholds for progression to the subsequent semester, are prescribed in the respective program curriculum document issued by the concerned School and are not restated in this general regulation.

## 1.5 Course Registration Procedure

Course registration for each semester is conducted through the student portal during the registration window notified in the Academic Calendar. A Registered Student is required to select courses for the upcoming semester from the list of courses notified as available by the concerned Department, subject to seat availability and any prerequisite requirements applicable to the course under Section 1.7. Course registration is confirmed only upon successful submission through the student portal; a student who begins but does not complete the registration process within the window is treated as unregistered for the affected courses.

## 1.6 Add/Drop Period

Following the close of the regular course registration window, an add/drop period of one week is provided during which a student may add a course to their registration, subject to seat availability, or drop a previously registered course without academic penalty. A course dropped during the add/drop period does not appear on the student's academic record. Requests to add or drop a course after the close of the add/drop period are not processed except as provided under Section 1.9.

## 1.7 Prerequisites

Certain courses are designated by the concerned Department as carrying one or more prerequisite courses, which a student must have completed and passed before registering for the course in question. The list of prerequisite courses for a given course is notified in the program curriculum document maintained by the concerned School. A student who registers for a course without having satisfied the applicable prerequisite is liable to have that registration cancelled by the Head of Department upon verification.

## 1.8 Credit Requirements and Academic Advising

A student is required to complete the minimum credit threshold prescribed for their program in order to progress to the subsequent semester and, eventually, to qualify for the award of the degree. Each student is assigned a Faculty Advisor by the concerned Department at the commencement of their program, who is available during notified consultation hours to advise the student on course selection, credit planning, and academic progress. Consultation with the Faculty Advisor is recommended but not mandatory prior to course registration, except in the case of a student who has been placed under academic probation, for whom advisor consultation is compulsory prior to registration for the subsequent semester.

## 1.9 Withdrawal from Individual Courses

A student who wishes to withdraw from an individually registered course after the close of the add/drop period, but before the commencement of the end-semester examination period, may apply for course withdrawal to the Head of Department, stating the reason for withdrawal. A course withdrawal approved under this provision is recorded on the student's academic record with the notation "Withdrawn" and does not affect the student's grade point average, but does count toward the maximum permissible number of withdrawals in a program, which is limited to four courses over the duration of the program.

## 1.10 Academic Records and Grade Publication

The academic record of each Registered Student, comprising grades obtained in each course, credits earned, and semester grade point average, is maintained by the Office of the Registrar and is made available to the student through the student portal upon publication. Grades for a semester are published within four weeks of the conclusion of the end-semester examination period for that semester, save where results are withheld pending resolution of a pending disciplinary or examination matter concerning the student. A student may request an official transcript of their academic record from the Office of the Registrar upon payment of the prescribed transcript fee.

## 1.11 Semester Completion Requirements

A semester is considered successfully completed by a student where the student has satisfied the applicable attendance requirement under Section 2.1, cleared the semester fee under Section 4.1, and obtained a passing grade in the minimum number of credits prescribed for that semester under the program curriculum. A student who does not satisfy the semester completion requirements is placed under academic probation for the subsequent semester, during which compulsory advisor consultation under Section 1.8 applies and the student's course load may be restricted by the Head of Department.

## 1.12 Course Load Limits

A student's course load for a semester is subject to a minimum and maximum credit limit prescribed by the concerned School, with the standard load ordinarily comprising 20 to 24 credits as noted under Section 1.1. A student wishing to register for a course load below the minimum or above the maximum prescribed limit must obtain prior written approval from the Head of Department, which is granted only in exceptional circumstances such as backlog clearance or approved credit overload for early completion of the program.

## 1.13 Notification of Academic Calendar Amendments

Where the Office of the Registrar amends any date specified in the Academic Calendar under Section 1.2, including examination dates, registration windows, or vacation periods, the amendment is notified to all Registered Students through the student portal and University email at least one week in advance, save where the amendment arises from circumstances beyond the University's control, in which case notification is provided at the earliest practicable opportunity. An amendment to the Academic Calendar does not, by itself, extend or reduce any deadline prescribed elsewhere in this rulebook unless the notification expressly states otherwise.

# 4. Fee Payment and Deadlines

## 4.1 Semester Fee Payment

Every Registered Student is required to pay the prescribed semester fee, comprising tuition fee, examination fee, library fee, and applicable hostel fee where relevant, in accordance with the fee structure notified by the Finance Office at the commencement of each Academic Year. The semester fee deadline for the Even Semester 2027 is 10 March 2027. Payment may be made through the University's online payment portal or at the designated fee counter during working hours.

A student who does not clear the semester fee by the applicable deadline is liable to late fee charges as prescribed under the fee schedule maintained by the Finance Office, and continued non-payment beyond the extended window may result in withholding of examination admission or cancellation of hostel allotment, as applicable. Fee payment confirmation is issued electronically and must be retained by the student as proof of payment for the semester.

# 9. General Provisions

## 9.1 Student Responsibilities

Every Registered Student is expected to acquaint themselves with the provisions of this rulebook and all policy documents issued under it, including the Attendance Policy, Examination Rules, Hostel Rules, and Scholarship Policy. Ignorance of a published rule does not, by itself, constitute grounds for exemption from its application. Students are responsible for monitoring official University communication channels, including the student portal and University email, for notices affecting their academic standing, fee status, and examination eligibility.

## 9.2 Amendment and Interpretation

The Competent Authority reserves the right to amend, modify, or supplement any provision of this rulebook at any time, with such amendment taking effect from the date notified. Where any provision of this rulebook is ambiguous or silent on a particular matter, the interpretation of the Competent Authority shall be sought, and such interpretation shall be recorded in writing and communicated to the concerned student or body of students.

## 9.3 Applicability

These regulations apply to all Registered Students of MediTech University across all Schools and programs for the Academic Year 2026–27, and remain in force until superseded by a subsequently notified version. Where a specific School or program has notified supplementary rules consistent with this rulebook, such supplementary rules apply in addition to, and not in substitution of, the provisions contained herein.
