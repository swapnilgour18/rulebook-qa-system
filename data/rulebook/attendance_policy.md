# 2. Attendance Policy

## 2.1 Minimum Attendance Requirement

All students must maintain a minimum attendance of 75% in each registered course.

## 2.2 Attendance Calculation

Attendance percentage for a course is calculated as the number of instructional sessions attended by the student divided by the total number of instructional sessions conducted for that course up to the relevant cut-off date, expressed as a percentage and rounded to the nearest whole number. The attendance cut-off date for each semester is notified in the Academic Calendar and ordinarily falls one week prior to the commencement of end-semester examinations. Attendance records are maintained by the concerned Department and are made available to students through the student portal on a fortnightly basis. A student who identifies a discrepancy in recorded attendance must report the discrepancy to the Head of Department in writing within one week of the discrepancy being reflected on the portal, accompanied by supporting evidence where available.

## 2.3 Medical Attendance Exemption

Students with an approved medical exemption must maintain a minimum attendance of 60% in each registered course.

## 2.4 Procedure for Medical Exemption

A student seeking a medical attendance exemption must submit an application to the Head of Department within two weeks of resuming attendance following the medical condition, or, where the condition persists through the attendance cut-off date, no later than the cut-off date itself. The application must be accompanied by a medical certificate issued by a registered medical practitioner, specifying the nature and duration of the condition, and, where the absence exceeds seven consecutive days, a certificate from a Government hospital or a hospital recognized by the University. The Head of Department is required to communicate a decision on the application within ten working days of receipt. A student aggrieved by the decision of the Head of Department may pursue the grievance procedure set out in Section 8.

## 2.5 Leave and Absence

Students are permitted to seek leave of absence from instructional sessions for reasons including participation in University-sponsored academic, cultural, or sporting events, bereavement in the immediate family, and personal medical conditions not amounting to hospitalization. Leave applications must be submitted to the Head of Department in advance where the reason for leave is known beforehand, and within three working days of resumption where the leave was unplanned. Approved leave is recorded against the student's attendance file for reference but does not, by itself, alter the attendance percentage calculated under Section 2.2 unless the leave falls within the scope of a medical exemption approved under Section 2.3.

## 2.6 Consequences of Attendance Shortfall

A student who does not meet the applicable attendance threshold for a course, and who has not been granted relief under Section 2.3, is debarred from the end-semester examination in that course and is treated as having a Detained status for that course for the relevant semester. A student with Detained status in a course must repeat the course in a subsequent semester in which it is offered, and is not eligible for supplementary examination in that course under Section 3.7 until the course has been repeated. The list of students with Detained status is notified by the Head of Department prior to the commencement of end-semester examinations and is communicated individually to affected students through the student portal.

## 2.7 Attendance Recording Procedure

Attendance for each instructional session is recorded by the concerned faculty member at the time the session is conducted, through the attendance module of the student portal or through a physical attendance register subsequently transferred to the portal by the Department office. Attendance so recorded is treated as the official record for the purposes of Section 2.1 and Section 2.2 unless successfully corrected under Section 2.8.

## 2.8 Attendance Correction Requests

A student who believes that their recorded attendance for a session is incorrect may submit an attendance correction request to the concerned faculty member within one week of the session in question, supported by any relevant evidence of presence, such as a signed acknowledgment from the faculty member or documentary proof of participation in an approved University activity during the session. The concerned faculty member is required to respond to the correction request within five working days, either by amending the attendance record or by providing written reasons for declining the correction. A student dissatisfied with the outcome of an attendance correction request may escalate the matter to the Head of Department.

## 2.9 Departmental Responsibilities

Each Department is responsible for ensuring that attendance records for courses offered by that Department are updated on the student portal on a fortnightly basis in accordance with Section 2.2, and for designating a faculty coordinator responsible for addressing attendance-related queries from students. The Head of Department is required to review aggregate attendance data for all courses offered by the Department at least once each month during the semester, in order to identify students at risk of attendance shortfall under Section 2.1 and to notify such students accordingly under Section 2.10.

## 2.10 Attendance Notification Procedures

Where a student's attendance in a course falls below 80% at any point during the semester, the concerned Department is required to notify the student through the student portal and University email, drawing attention to the applicable minimum attendance requirement under Section 2.1 and the medical exemption provision under Section 2.3. Such notification is intended to alert the student to the risk of attendance shortfall and does not, by itself, constitute a determination of Detained status, which is made only in accordance with Section 2.6 following the attendance cut-off date under Section 2.2.

## 2.11 Attendance and Course Withdrawal

Where a student withdraws from a course in accordance with Section 1.9, attendance recorded for that course up to the date of withdrawal is not carried forward and does not affect the student's attendance percentage in any other registered course. A student who re-registers for a withdrawn course in a subsequent semester is required to satisfy the attendance requirement under Section 2.1 afresh for that course, calculated independently of attendance recorded during the earlier, withdrawn registration.
